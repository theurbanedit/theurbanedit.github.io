This policy explains what information is processed when you visit The Urban Edit ("we", "us"). We have tried to keep it short and plain.

## What we collect

**We do not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies or tracking pixels.** The site is a static website and does not ask you to create an account.

- **Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operation of the service. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Fonts.** Our typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Search.** Site search runs entirely in your browser. What you type is not sent to us.
- **Sharing links.** Share buttons are plain links. Nothing is loaded from social networks until you click one.

## Newsletter

The newsletter is not live yet and the sign-up form does not collect or send anything. When it launches, your email address will be processed by the email provider we use solely to send you the newsletter, and you will be able to unsubscribe at any time. We will update this policy to name the provider before sign-ups open.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Contact us via the [contact page]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).

## Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.
