## Corrections

If you spot a factual error, tell us which article, what is wrong and, ideally, a source. We correct errors promptly and note significant corrections at the end of the article. See our [editorial policy]({{ '/editorial-policy/' | relative_url }}).

## Pitches

We welcome pitches from writers, designers and photographers. Send a short outline (three to five sentences), why now, and links to two or three examples of your published work.

## Partnerships

Any commercial partnership is separate from editorial decisions and is always clearly labelled.
