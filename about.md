The Urban Edit was founded in 2026 for people who think about how they live and work: entrepreneurs and professionals, remote workers and freelancers, students, creatives, and anyone curious about style, design and the way cities are changing.

We publish across twelve sections, from [Fashion & Style]({{ '/fashion/' | relative_url }}) and [Business & Entrepreneurship]({{ '/business/' | relative_url }}) to [Coworking & Workspaces]({{ '/coworking/' | relative_url }}), [Technology]({{ '/technology/' | relative_url }}), [Home & Interior Design]({{ '/home-design/' | relative_url }}) and [Culture]({{ '/culture/' | relative_url }}). What ties them together is a simple question: *what actually makes modern life better?*

## What we believe

- **Useful over loud.** We would rather publish one considered guide than ten quick takes.
- **Evidence matters.** When we state a fact, we link to where it comes from. When something is opinion, we say so.
- **Design is for everyone.** Good spaces, good clothes and good tools should not need insider knowledge.
- **Independence.** No article on The Urban Edit is paid for by the brands or businesses it mentions. If that ever changes for a piece, it will be clearly labelled.
