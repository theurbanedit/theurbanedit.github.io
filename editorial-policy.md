Readers should know how the stories they read are made. These are the standards every article on The Urban Edit is held to.

## Accuracy and sourcing

- Factual claims are linked to a primary or reputable source wherever possible, and articles list their key sources at the end.
- We do not invent statistics, quotations, experts or case studies.
- Opinion and analysis are written as such, not dressed up as fact.

## Independence

Editorial decisions are made by the editorial team alone. Brands and businesses cannot pay to be included in an article or to review one before publication. Any future sponsored content will be labelled clearly at the top of the page.

## Corrections

When we get something wrong, we fix it. Minor fixes (typos, broken links) are made silently; meaningful corrections are noted at the bottom of the article with the date. Report an error via our [contact page]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).

## Images

We only use images we have created, licensed, or are permitted to use, and every image carries descriptive alt text. Our launch edition uses original abstract illustrations made for the magazine.

## Use of AI tools

Some articles, including our launch collection, are prepared with AI-assisted drafting. Articles produced this way are labelled at the end with a "launch edition note", and they follow the same rules on sourcing and accuracy as everything else we publish.

## Updates

Evergreen guides are reviewed periodically. When an article is substantially updated, the "Updated" date beneath the headline changes.
